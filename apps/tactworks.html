What it writes

Four kinds of business email

Cold outreach

First contact with a new prospect.

Sales emails

Clear pitches with a specific call to action.

Reminder emails

From a gentle nudge to an urgent follow-up.

Apology emails

From a minor slip to a serious issue.

How it works

From a few fields to a finished email

  1. Pick the email type

    Choose cold outreach, sales, reminder or apology.

  2. Fill in a few short fields

    Choose a tone or urgency level. Add the recipient's name and your own name, and they go straight into the email.

  3. Generate, review, send

    You get a complete email with a subject line, greeting and sign-off, ready to review and send.

FAQ

Questions about Tactworks

No. Tactworks does not require an account.

Where does my text go?

Everything you type stays on your device, and emails are written on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Tactworks is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.0 or later.
