Draftmill: from one topic
to everything you publish

Ten content tools in one app. Plan what to write, then write what you publish: outlines, ideas, headlines, video titles, descriptions, captions and hooks, all in one place.

Download on the App Store → Free to download · In-app subscriptions · iPhone & iPad · iOS 15.0 or later
What's inside

Ten tools for planning and writing content

Blog Outline

Pick a style (listicle, how-to, story, comparison or deep-dive), set the tone and 3 to 10 sections, and get a full heading structure for your post.

Blog Ideas

Describe your niche, pick a content goal, and get 5 to 15 post ideas.

Content Ideas

Choose blog, social or video and get 5 to 12 content angles and formats.

Headline Writer

3 to 10 headlines in five styles for articles, blogs, videos, emails and ads.

Video Title Writer

5 to 12 titles in five patterns, from curiosity to how-to.

Video Description Writer

A full description with chapters and tags, with optional timestamps.

Captions by Length

Social posts written to the length you pick, from 80 to 280 characters.

Captions & Hashtags

A photo caption plus a matching hashtag set, in five moods.

Short-Video Captions

Three caption options for vertical video, with 0 to 8 hashtags.

Video Hooks

8 openers for the first three seconds, each with a spoken line and an on-screen cue.

How it works

Pick a tool, enter a topic, copy the result

  1. Pick a tool

    Choose one of the ten tools for what you are writing today.

  2. Enter your topic and options

    Set the options, such as style, tone, length or number of results, and tap Generate.

  3. Copy, or come back later

    Your result is ready to copy with one tap, and every result is saved to History on your device.

Privacy

Private by design

  • No account required. Everything you type stays on your device.
  • Results are saved to History on your device.
  • Writing features work offline once installed. An internet connection is required for App Store purchases and restoring purchases.

Payments are handled by Apple. To manage in-app purchases and subscriptions, AIJJ apps use RevenueCat, as described in section 7 of our Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Questions about Draftmill

Who is Draftmill for?

Bloggers, video creators and anyone who posts on social media and wants a faster start on outlines, titles, captions and hooks.

Can I find an earlier result again?

Yes. Every result is saved to History on your device, so you can come back and copy it again later.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Writing features work offline once installed. An internet connection is required for App Store purchases and restoring purchases.

Which devices are supported?

Draftmill is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.0 or later.
