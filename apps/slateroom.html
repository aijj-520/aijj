Slateroom: course outlines
and lesson plans, ready to teach

Two planning tools for teachers, in one app. Build a complete course outline or prepare a single lesson. Enter your topic or course title, set the scope, and your structured plan appears.

Download on the App Store → Free to download · In-app subscriptions · iPhone & iPad · iOS 15.0 or later
What it creates

Two tools for planning a course or a class

Course Outline

An overview, learning objectives, a module-by-module breakdown, session topics, hands-on activities, assessment ideas and prerequisites.

From 3 to 12 modules, from one week to a full semester, at beginner, intermediate or advanced level.

Lesson Plan

One class in five phases: warm-up, direct instruction, guided practice, independent practice and wrap-up.

A minute-by-minute split that matches your class length exactly, plus the materials you need and differentiation ideas for students who struggle and for those who are ahead.

How it works

From a topic to a structured plan

  1. Choose a tool

    Course Outline for a whole course, or Lesson Plan for a single class.

  2. Enter your topic and scope

    Type your topic or course title, then set the scope, such as the number of modules, the duration or the level.

  3. Teach from the plan

    Each lesson phase shows what the teacher does, what students do, and gives a concrete prompt or example.

Privacy

Your course details stay with you

  • All processing happens on your device. Your course details are never sent to a server.
  • No account required. Everything you type stays on your device.
  • Writing features work offline once installed. An internet connection is required for App Store purchases and restoring purchases.

Payments are handled by Apple. To manage in-app purchases and subscriptions, AIJJ apps use RevenueCat, as described in section 7 of our Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Questions about Slateroom

Who is Slateroom for?

Teachers and trainers who want a structured course outline or a timed lesson plan for a topic they teach.

How long can a lesson be?

You set the class length, and the five phases are split minute by minute to match it exactly.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. Writing features work offline once installed. An internet connection is required for App Store purchases and restoring purchases.

Which devices are supported?

Slateroom is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad running iOS 15.0 or later.
