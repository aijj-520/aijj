What you get

Two PDFs, ready to print

Student worksheets

One page per student, with a name or student number, a date line and the problems. Every worksheet in the class has its own set of problems, and the worksheets never show answers.

Teacher answer key

A separate PDF with every student’s answers, listed by student number and name, so you can grade each sheet against its own key.

A worksheet code

Printed on every page. Enter it later to reprint the whole class or a single student’s worksheet with exactly the same problems.

How it works

Three steps to printable worksheets

  1. Choose the worksheet

    Addition, subtraction, multiplication or division; Easy, Medium or Hard; 10, 20 or 30 problems per student; US Letter or A4 paper.

  2. Add your students

    Enter a class size from 1 to 40, or paste a list of names, one per line, for example a column copied from a spreadsheet.

  3. Download and print

    Download the student worksheets and the answer key, then print at actual size (100%), one-sided, so each sheet belongs to one student.

Levels

What each level includes

AdditionEasy: 1–9 + 1–9. Medium: two-digit + two-digit, half with regrouping. Hard: three-digit + three-digit, with one or two regroupings.
SubtractionEasy: facts within 18, answers 0–9. Medium: two-digit − two-digit, half with borrowing. Hard: three-digit − three-digit, with borrowing, including across zero.
MultiplicationEasy: 1–5 × 1–10. Medium: 2–12 × 2–12. Hard: two-digit × one-digit.
DivisionEasy: divisors 1–5, answers 1–10. Medium: divisors and answers 2–12. Hard: two- or three-digit ÷ one-digit, with two-digit answers.

Subtraction answers are never negative and division always comes out even. Each problem number is the same kind of problem on every worksheet (for example, problem 5 might be two-digit addition with regrouping); only the numbers change, so some combinations may still feel easier or harder to an individual student.

Privacy

Student names stay in your browser

  • No account and no sign-up.
  • Names you type are used only to print the name line. They are not uploaded or saved by the generator, and they are cleared when you close or reload the page.
  • Worksheets and answer keys are built as PDFs on your device. After the generator page loads, it makes no network requests and works offline.
  • The generator page uses no cookies, analytics, ads or trackers. Like any website, the server that delivers the page may keep basic connection records, such as IP address and time; those never include student names or worksheet content.

The generator has its own privacy notice on its page. For this website, see our Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Questions teachers ask

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no account, no sign-up and nothing to buy.

Does every student really get different problems?

Yes. No two worksheets in a class are identical. On Medium and Hard, two students share the same problem at the same question number on no more than 1 in 10 questions. Easy has fewer possible problems, so repeats between students are more common there.

How do I print the same worksheets again?

Open “Reprint from worksheet code” in the generator, enter the code printed at the bottom of every worksheet, and reprint the whole class or one student. Use the same class size or the same name list, in the same order.

Which names can I use?

Names with English letters, accented letters (such as José or Zoë), Vietnamese letters, apostrophes, hyphens and periods, up to 30 characters and up to 40 names. Other scripts and emoji can’t be printed yet; the generator tells you which name to change.

Does it work on iPhone, iPad and Android?

Yes, in the phone’s web browser. On iPhone or iPad, Safari may open the PDF instead of downloading it: tap Share, then Save to Files or Print. On Android, the PDF opens or appears in your downloads list.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes, once the generator page has loaded. Worksheets and PDFs are created on your device.
